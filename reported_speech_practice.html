Reported Speech Reference Guide

Based on Passages 1

When reporting what someone said, we usually shift the tense back in time (**backshift**), change pronouns, and adjust time/place words. Review the rules below before trying the exercises!

1. Reported Statements

Present Simple → Past Simple
"It's a big secret."
➔ He said (that) it was a big secret.
Present Cont. → Past Cont.
"I'm getting a terrible grade."
➔ He said (that) he was getting a terrible grade.
Past Simple / Pres. Perf. → Past Perfect
"They got engaged." / "She's been absent..."
➔ He said they had gotten engaged. / had been absent.
Future (Will) → Would
"I'll meet you at the café."
➔ He said he would meet me at the café.

2. Reported Questions

Yes/No Questions (Use 'if' or 'whether')
"Did you know about the layoffs?"
➔ I asked him if he had known about the layoffs.
Wh- Questions (Use normal statement word order)
"What are you saying?"
➔ I asked them what they were saying. (Not: what were they)

3. Commands & 4. General Truths

Commands (Use infinitives: to / not to)
"Don't say anything!"
➔ He warned his friend not to say anything.
General Truths / Scientific Facts (No Tense Shift!)
"The sun rises in the east."
➔ She said that the sun rises in the east.
